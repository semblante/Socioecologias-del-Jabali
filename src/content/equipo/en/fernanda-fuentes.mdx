UC sociologist and MA student in Sociology at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. Interested in environmental sociology, science and technology studies (STS), and relations among community, territory, and environment. My work studies socioecological relations through qualitative methods, with special interest in situated knowledge production, environmental governance, and forms of coexistence addressing territory, community, and identity. Thesis title within the project: “Technoscientific construction of *Sus scrofa* as an invasive species and territorial management in the Andean Araucanía.”
