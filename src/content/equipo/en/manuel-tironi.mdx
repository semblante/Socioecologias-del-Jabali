Full professor at the Institute for Sustainable Development and the Institute of Sociology, UC. Principal investigator at the UC Center for Local Development (CEDEL) in Villarrica and at Cigiden R+. His work crosses social studies of science, cultural anthropology, and environmental humanities to investigate how ways of understanding relations with more-than-human entities and processes are formed, validated, and transformed. In recent years his agenda has centered on collaborations with different collectives, mainly Indigenous and campesino, to think with them forms of action and reflection in situations of ecological risk and harm.
