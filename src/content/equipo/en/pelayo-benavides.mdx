Associate professor at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, Villarrica campus, and Principal Investigator at the UC Center for Local Development (CEDEL). His research centers on environmental anthropology and human–animal relations. In education, he has taken part in research on traditional Māori narratives as educational tools; intercultural education in Mapuche contexts; and, more recently, perceptions of outdoor activities and relations with nature among children in local schools.
