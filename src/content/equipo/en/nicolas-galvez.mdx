Associate professor at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, Villarrica campus, and Principal Investigator at the UC Center for Local Development (CEDEL). He has spent more than 12 years researching wildlife and doing outreach. His work focuses on southern Chile and carnivore conservation, particularly the small güiña cat (*Leopardus guigna*). His main research interests concern how habitat loss and fragmentation, and how human–animal interactions, can affect wildlife and vice versa. Ultimately, the research and activities aim at how to advance toward coexistence.
