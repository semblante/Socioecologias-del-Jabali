Winkulche Pehuenche from the María Cayulef Viuda de Huilipan Community. Member of the Wildlife Group of the Winkulmapu Indigenous Association. He is in charge of Administration, Finance, and Operations of the Füta Mawiza Kurarewe Project. Management engineer with a certificate in Territorial Governance and Climate Change. Member of the Mapuche Territorial Mesa of Pucón and the Koyawtun of Kurarewe (Mapuche Territorial Parliament of Kurarewe), and coordinator of the Colo Colo Urban Wetland environmental-territorial initiative in Curarrehue.
