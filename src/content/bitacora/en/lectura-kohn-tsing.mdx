As a theoretical starting point, the team reviewed texts by Eduardo Kohn (*How Forests Think*)
and Anna Tsing (*The Mushroom at the End of the World*) to discuss how to think about wild
boar beyond the native/invasive binary.

The reading does not aim to apply frameworks mechanically, but to open vocabulary for
articulating Peweñantu's socioecologies in conversation with STS and environmental humanities.
