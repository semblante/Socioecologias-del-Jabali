To complement information gathered in this territory, the project includes Quiñenahuin, Panqui, and Palguín Alto as secondary cases. These sectors make up the broader peweñantu (pewen forest) of the Kurarewe cordillera —an ecological and political territory where Sus scrofa has been established for more than a century.
